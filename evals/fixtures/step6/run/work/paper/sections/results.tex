\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Table~\ref{tab:main} reports the throughput of every system at $90\%$ recall.
Quokka is the fastest system on every collection and the gap grows with the
collection size.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\caption{Throughput (QPS) at 90\% recall, best in bold.}
\label{tab:main}
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
System & Small & Large \\
Quokka & \textbf{1200} & \textbf{340} \\
Burrow & 1100 & 310 \\
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The speedup is
\begin{equation}
  s = \frac{1200}{1100} = 1.10,
\end{equation}
which is modest. The memory is measured with the resident set size.

\caption{Recall against throughput on the small collection.}
\label{fig:plot}
\end{figure}

Figure~\ref{fig:plot} shows the trade-off. A recall of 0.9 is reached by every system.
