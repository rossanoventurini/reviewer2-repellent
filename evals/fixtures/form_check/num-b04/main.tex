\section{Results}
Our model has 1.6M parameters and an index of 800k entries (Table~\ref{tab:p}).
The baseline needs \SI{640}{\milli\second} per query (Table~\ref{tab:t}), and
its learning rate is $10^{-3}$, while ours uses 1e-4 and $3\times10^{-4}$.
\begin{table}[h]\centering\caption{Model sizes.}\label{tab:p}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
Model & Params & Index & LR \\
\midrule
Base & 1.5M & 800k & 1e-3 \\
Ours & 1.5M & 800k & $10^{-3}$ \\
Big & 12M & 2.4M & $3\times10^{-4}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\end{table}

\end{tabular}\end{table}
