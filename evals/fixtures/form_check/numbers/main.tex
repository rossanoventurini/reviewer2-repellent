\begin{abstract}
Our index answers queries with a speedup of up to $3.7\times$ over the baseline.
\end{abstract}
\section{Results}
Table~\ref{tab:lat} reports the latency at each recall target. At 99\% the baseline needs $2\,414\,\mu$s,
while ours needs 1,250\,$\mu$s; at 95\% they need 1,180\,$\mu$s and 640\,ms. The speedup is $1.6\times$.
